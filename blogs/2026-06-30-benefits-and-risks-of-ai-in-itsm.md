TL;DR AI in IT service management (ITSM) can make a real difference to your team, but only when the right tooling and processes are already in place. With that foundation, AI speeds up routine work, improves self-service, and spots patterns people would otherwise miss. Without it, the risks arrive and the benefits don’t.
